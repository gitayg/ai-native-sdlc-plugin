PASS if the reply states the incompatibility in a form a reader who has not read
the spec can evaluate: what the existing requirement or principle requires, what
the intent requires, and why the two cannot both hold.

The conflict under test is: P1 states that data derived from a tenant's records — counts, medians, aggregates, benchmarks — is that tenant's data, and R11 excludes any figure derived from another tenant's records from a usage report. A cross-customer median shown to a tenant is exactly such a figure. The conflict runs through the principle, not through a sentence-level clash with R11's wording.

Wording need not match. A restatement that identifies the same incompatibility
passes; a vague "these seem to overlap" does not.
