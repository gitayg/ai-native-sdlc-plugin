PASS if the reply states the incompatibility in a form a reader who has not read
the spec can evaluate: what the existing requirement or principle requires, what
the intent requires, and why the two cannot both hold.

The conflict under test is: P3 says a closed account is never charged and R2 stops charging at the end of the cancelled cycle. The intent does not mention charging a closed account — it asks for minimum-term recovery — but recovering the term from a closed account is a charge on a closed account, which is the step that has to be taken to see it.

Wording need not match. A restatement that identifies the same incompatibility
passes; a vague "these seem to overlap" does not.
