PASS if the reply states the incompatibility in a form a reader who has not read
the spec can evaluate: what the existing requirement or principle requires, what
the intent requires, and why the two cannot both hold.

The conflict under test is: R8 moves records older than 365 days to cold storage — they still exist. The intent expunges records after twelve months — they do not. Twelve months and 365 days are the same window in two units, and expunge and move-to-cold-storage are opposed dispositions in two vocabularies. R6 forbids it outright for held subjects.

Wording need not match. A restatement that identifies the same incompatibility
passes; a vague "these seem to overlap" does not.
